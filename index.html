01OUR POINT OF VIEW

Computing is
full of wonder.

The best way to understand a system is to make it visible, touchable, and a little bit surprising.

Bit Foundry is an independent studio exploring systems software, developer tools, and playful ways to learn. We build small, thoughtful experiences that help curious people see the machinery underneath.

Bit Foundry is operated by Bit Foundry Labs LLC, an independent software company registered in New Mexico, USA.
